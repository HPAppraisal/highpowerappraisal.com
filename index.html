Services · themed by situation

What do you need the number to do?

An appraisal is ordered for a reason. Each service below starts with that reason — who it is for, when the value date falls, and what the report has to hold up to. Scope and fee are confirmed before any assignment begins.

01 · PURCHASE & REFINANCE

The lending decision

The core of residential practice: a well-supported market value for a purchase or refinance, produced on current forms in ACI and delivered through your normal ordering channel.

  • Single-family, condo, townhome, and new construction
  • Current-form production today; UAD 3.6 coursework complete
  • Ordered through lender and AMC panels — see For Lenders & AMCs
02 · ESTATE & DATE-OF-DEATH

Settling an estate

When someone passes, the value that matters is often the value on the date of death — a retrospective appraisal that supports step-up in basis and estate filings, handled with care for the family and the file.

  • Retrospective value as of the date of death
  • Support for estate and tax filings (Forms 706/709 context)
  • Works directly with executors, attorneys, and CPAs
03 · DIVORCE

One neutral number

In a divorce, the house is usually the largest shared asset — and the value has to be neutral, documented, and defensible for both sides, whether the date of value is current or retrospective.

  • Independent, unbiased — engaged by either party or jointly
  • Current or retrospective dates of value
  • Clear documentation for settlement or court use
04 · TAX APPEAL / IRS

Challenging an assessment

If a Clark County assessment looks higher than the market supports, an independent appraisal gives your appeal evidence instead of opinion — built to USPAP standards and explained in plain language.

  • Independent market evidence for assessment appeals
  • Clark County process context, including Board of Equalization filings
  • IRS-related valuation inquiries scoped case by case
05 · PRE-LISTING & PMI REMOVAL

Before you list — or to drop PMI

Price a listing on evidence instead of hope, or document the value needed to remove private mortgage insurance. Includes careful ANSI-standard measurement, so the living area in the report matches the house.

  • Pre-listing and pre-purchase private valuations
  • PMI-removal documentation for your loan servicer
  • ANSI Z765 measurement — trained and current
06 · REO & RELOCATION

Distressed and moving-fast work

REO assignments and relocation-related valuations, where condition, market exposure, and timing all shape the number — and where local knowledge of each submarket does the heavy lifting.

  • REO experience across Las Vegas, North Las Vegas, Henderson, and the surrounding incorporated valley communities
  • Condition and marketability documented in the report
  • Vacant and hard-to-access properties scoped up front

Fee schedule

Standard private assignments.

Fees are based on the complexity and scope of the assignment — the property's size, condition, and location, and the time and work the assignment requires — never on the value of the property or the outcome of the appraisal. Your exact fee is quoted and confirmed in writing before work begins.

Single-family appraisal Form 1004 · Full interior

A full interior inspection and appraisal of a single-family home — the standard report for purchase, refinance, pre-listing, and private decisions.

$450–$550

1004 New Construction (plans & specs)

Built into the full 1004 appraisal — not an add-on. Covers the as-is value and, when the home is subject to completion per plans and specs, the initial plans-and-specs inspection with a completion percentage, plus the additional comparable support inside and outside the subdivision the assignment requires.

$500–$600

Condominium appraisal Form 1073 · Full interior

A full interior inspection and appraisal of a condominium unit, including the project and common-area context the value depends on.

$450–$550

Exterior-only appraisal Form 2055 · Drive-by

An exterior-only observation with a full market analysis — no interior access — for situations where a drive-by scope is appropriate.

$350–$400

Condominium exterior-only Form 1075 · Drive-by

The exterior-only scope applied to a condominium unit — observed from outside, supported by the same market research.

$350–$400

Private appraisal (GPAR) Divorce · Probate & estate

Private work for divorce, probate and estate matters, and date-of-death valuations at a current date of value.

$300–$500
